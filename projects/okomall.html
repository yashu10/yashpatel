UX case study

OKOMALL — Salon Discovery & Booking

A responsive platform where people find salons, compare services and prices, pick a professional and send a booking request in four steps.

Type
UX case study · Web app
Role
UI/UX Designer
Platform
Responsive web (desktop and mobile)
Tools
Figma

View more on Behance ↗

The problem

Why it needed designing

Booking a salon still means calls, DMs and guesswork. Prices are unclear, availability is unknown and there is no single place to track appointments.

The solution

What I designed

OKOMALL lets people discover salons near them, compare services and prices, choose a professional and a time slot, and send a booking request, all in four steps.

Who it is for

Customer

Wants to find a nearby salon quickly, see honest prices and ratings, and book without calling.

Salon owner

Wants more bookings, a clear list of requests and a way to promote offers and free slots.

Design goals

Fewer steps to book, trust through ratings and clear prices, and the same experience on desktop and mobile.

Key design decisions

Search leads the home page

Service, area and time sit in the hero, so the main task is the first thing people see.

Free today near you

Salons with open slots today come first, turning urgency into a booking.

Four-step booking with a live summary

A progress bar and a summary card stay visible, so people always know what they chose and what it costs.

Selected screens

Outcome

Home, search, salon pages, offers, help, a four-step booking flow and account screens, each in desktop and mobile, built on one design system.

What I would do next

Usability-test the booking flow with real customers, design the salon-owner dashboard, and add payment and reminders.
