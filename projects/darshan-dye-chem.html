Darshan Dye-Chem — Website Design

A clear, trustworthy website for an Ahmedabad dye manufacturer: product families, shade cards, dye tools and an easy way to ask for a quote. Designed in Figma for desktop and mobile, and live on the web.

The brief

Why it needed designing

Dye buyers want proof before they ask for a quote: what the company makes, which shades exist, how reliable the supply is, and how to get a sample. The website had to answer those questions quickly for a business that sells to textile, leather, paper and industrial buyers across India and overseas.

The solution

What I designed

A dark, confident look with warm amber actions. Visitors see the product families, an online shade explorer, downloadable shade cards and free dye tools, with a Get a Quote button always within reach. The same system works on desktop and mobile.

Key design decisions

Trust first

The home page leads with why buyers choose the company: consistent shades, direct from factory, export ready and sample first.

See the shades before you ask

Shade cards, an online shade explorer with filters and fastness ratings, and PDF downloads let buyers check colours without a phone call.

Tools that help buyers decide

A dye calculator and a dye finder estimate quantity and suggest a dye family, which brings useful visitors to the quote form.

Pages

Home (desktop)

About

A founder’s message, company facts, a laboratory gallery, milestones from 2000 to today and the team, all in one page that builds confidence in a proprietor-led manufacturer.

Products

A shade explorer with search and filters, a clear list of other colourants, downloadable shade cards and guidance on choosing between acid and direct dyes.

Contact

Address, contact person, phone and email on one side, and a short enquiry form on the other, so asking for a quote takes under a minute.

Mobile

The home page redesigned for phones: one column, full-width buttons and large photo cards.

Design system

Colours, typography and core components used across the site.

Outcome

A complete four-page website (Home, About, Products and Contact) with a mobile layout and a small design system, live at darshandyechem.com.
