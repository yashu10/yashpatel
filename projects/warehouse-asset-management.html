UX case study

Warehouse Asset Management

A mobile app that helps warehouse teams add, locate, verify and back up assets, with scanning built into every step.

Type
UX case study · Mobile app
Role
UI/UX Designer
Platform
Mobile app
Tools
Figma

View more on Behance ↗

The problem

Why it needed designing

In a large warehouse, assets are logged on paper or spreadsheets. Locations are inconsistent, checks take long, and a mismatch between records and reality is found too late.

The solution

What I designed

A mobile app where the team adds assets by scanning, assigns each to a clear location (tower, floor, section), verifies them in bulk, and exports and backs up records from a phone on the warehouse floor.

Who it is for

Warehouse operator

Adds and verifies assets while walking the floor, often with one hand and little time per item.

Supervisor / admin

Manages users, locations and backups, and needs to trust that records match reality.

Design goals

Scan first and type last, few fields per screen, large tap targets and clear status colours.

Key design decisions

One dashboard, five jobs

Large tiles for All Assets, Notifications, Location Master, User Master and Backup mean nobody has to learn a menu.

Scan-first entry

Scanning an EPC tag fills the asset identity, with manual entry available when a tag is missing.

Bulk verification

Detected tags appear in one list with filters (All, New, Valid, Missing), so many items are confirmed without opening each one.

Selected screens

Outcome

Onboarding, login, dashboard, user, asset, verification, location and backup flows in one consistent mobile system.

What I would do next

Test the bulk scan flow with operators on a real warehouse floor, add offline support and design a tablet layout for supervisors.
