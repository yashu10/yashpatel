Industrial machinery website

A five-page corporate website (Home, Products, About, Blog and Contact) for a mineral water filling and capping machinery manufacturer. A navy and red palette, a product-led layout and a clear Get in Touch block make a technical catalogue feel trustworthy.

Real estate website

A home page for a premium apartment developer (client work). Warm copper and dark brown tones signal luxury, and a search panel in the hero gets visitors to relevant properties fast, followed by stats, amenities, apartment plans, testimonials and a map.

Landing pages

Landing pages for skincare, e-commerce and education. Each has a clear hero, focused sections and strong calls to action, built to guide visitors to one goal.

Outcome

Responsive layouts with clear hierarchy, strong calls to action and consistent visual systems for each industry.
