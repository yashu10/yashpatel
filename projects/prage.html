UX case study

PRAGE — EV Bike Rental App

A mobile app for renting electric bikes, from finding a nearby hub and verifying documents to payment, ride tracking and support.

Type
UX case study · Mobile app
Role
UI/UX Designer
Platform
iOS / Android
Tools
Figma

View more on Behance ↗

The problem

Why it needed designing

Renting an electric bike should feel as simple as unlocking a phone. In practice, riders face unclear availability, repeated paperwork and uncertainty about battery, range and charges.

The solution

What I designed

PRAGE brings everything into one app: find the nearest hub, see vehicle details and rental plans, verify identity once, pay, start and end the trip with photo checks, and get support when needed.

Research and thinking

  1. 01

    Problem and solution statement

    Defined who the product is for and what has to be solved first.

  2. 02

    Competitive analysis

    Looked at ride-hailing apps and local rental services to find gaps and patterns.

  3. 03

    Empathy map and persona

    Turned research into a clear picture of the rider, their needs and frustrations.

  4. 04

    SWOT, mind map and card sorting

    Organised features into groups that match how riders think.

  5. 05

    User flow

    Mapped the full journey from onboarding to refund and rating.

Key design decisions

Hub-first discovery

The home screen is a map with the nearest hubs, distance and a Navigate action, so riders start from where they are.

Transparent vehicle details

Battery, range, speed, seating and rental plans (such as 3-day and 5-day) are shown up front, with extra charges stated clearly.

Step-by-step verification

Aadhaar, OTP, driving licence and selfie are split into four short steps with a progress indicator.

Selected screens

Outcome

A complete mobile experience covering onboarding, verification, hub discovery, booking, payment, trips, feedback, support and profile, backed by research and a clear user flow.

What I would do next

Usability-test the verification and trip start/end flows with real riders, and refine them based on what slows people down.
