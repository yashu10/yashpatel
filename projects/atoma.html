UX case study

Atoma — Home Beauty & Salon App

An at-home beauty and salon booking app with an AI beauty assistant, detailed service pages and a simple booking flow.

Type
UX case study · Mobile app
Role
UI/UX Designer
Platform
iOS
Tools
Figma

View more on Behance ↗

The problem

Why it needed designing

Booking beauty services at home usually means calls, messages and guesswork: what is included, how long it takes, what it costs, and which service fits your skin or hair.

The solution

What I designed

Atoma lets people browse services and products, read clear service details, get guidance from an AI assistant, and book a visit in a few taps.

Process

  1. 01

    User personas

    Defined the main customers and what they expect from at-home services.

  2. 02

    Workflow

    Mapped the complete flow from splash and home to cart, booking and confirmation.

  3. 03

    Wireframes

    Explored layouts for home, service detail, booking and the AI chat.

  4. 04

    High-fidelity UI and design system

    Built a navy and white visual system in SF Pro with reusable components.

Key design decisions

AI beauty assistant

A chat concierge gives personalised suggestions, so people do not have to guess which service to choose.

Detail pages that answer questions

Duration, rating, price and a clear description sit on the service page, with a strong Book action.

One simple booking flow

Cart, date and time, address and payment follow each other without detours, ending in a thank-you screen.

Selected screens

Outcome

A complete booking app covering discovery, service details, an AI assistant, cart and booking, with a documented design system for consistency.

What I would do next

Test the AI assistant suggestions with real customers and add reminders and rebooking.
