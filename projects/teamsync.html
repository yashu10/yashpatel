UX case study

TeamSync — Project Management Dashboard

A single workspace where teams see their projects, tasks, conversations and deadlines without switching tools.

Type
UX case study · Web dashboard
Role
UI/UX Designer
Platform
Web app (desktop)
Tools
Figma

View more on Behance ↗

The problem

Why it needed designing

Task lists live in one app, chat in another, files in a third and deadlines in a calendar nobody checks. Managers cannot see progress at a glance, and team members cannot tell what is due today.

The solution

What I designed

TeamSync brings projects, tasks, team chat, files and a shared calendar into one dashboard, so a team member opens it once and knows what matters today.

Who it is for

Team member

Needs to know what is due today, which tasks are in progress and where to talk to teammates.

Project manager

Needs overall progress, workload spread across teams and project health.

Design goals

Reduce tool switching, make status instantly readable and keep dense data calm.

Key design decisions

Summary first

Four headline numbers (active tasks, projects, due today, completed) make the state of the day clear in two seconds.

Visual progress

Progress bars, a task distribution ring and a gauge replace long tables, so workload is easy to scan.

Colour-coded Kanban

To Do, In-Progress and Completed each have their own colour, so status reads without labels.

Screens

Outcome

A consistent desktop workspace covering the dashboard, task board, calendar and profile menu, built on a reusable component set.

What I would do next

Usability-test the dashboard with real teams, add dark mode and design the Projects, Files and Planning screens.
