UX case study

InnerPeace — Mental Wellness App

A personalized mental wellness app that helps people understand their emotions, manage stress and build healthier daily habits.

Type
UX case study · Mobile app
Role
UI/UX Designer
Platform
iOS
Tools
Figma

View more on Behance ↗

The problem

Why it needed designing

Stress is easy to feel and hard to understand. Many wellness apps start with a blank screen or a long list of content, which is overwhelming for someone who is already tired.

The solution

What I designed

InnerPeace starts with a short, gentle onboarding to learn goals and feelings, builds a personal plan, then offers daily mood check-ins, guided sessions and a progress view in a calm, low-pressure interface.

Who it is for

Needs

A simple way to name how they feel, short guided sessions and proof that small daily habits are working.

Frustrations

Apps that feel clinical or crowded, too many choices at once and no sense of progress.

Design goals

Gentle onboarding, one clear action per screen, calm colour and soft shapes, visible progress.

Key design decisions

Six gentle steps to a personal plan

Goals, topics and short agree-or-disagree statements make reflection quick, and the flow ends with a reward.

Emoji mood check-in

Feelings are large tiles with a label, so choosing is quick and needs no typing.

A session with one cue

The guided session shows a single breathing cue, a timer and one pause button, nothing else.

Selected screens

Outcome

Onboarding, home, mood check-in, explore, guided sessions and progress in one soft, consistent mobile system.

What I would do next

Interview real users to validate the persona, test the onboarding length and add reminders and a profile screen.
