About me

I design products people understand at first glance.

I'm a UI/UX designer based in India. Over the years I've worked on mobile apps, dashboards, websites and brand identities for clients across several industries.

I like to start with the problem: who is using this, what are they trying to do, and what gets in their way. Then I design simple flows, clear screens and consistent systems in Figma.
